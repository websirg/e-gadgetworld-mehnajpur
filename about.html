Our Story

About E-Gadget World Mehnajpur

Dedicated to providing the residents and students of Mehnajpur, Azamgarh with high-performance refurbished laptops, authentic computer accessories, and prompt digital solutions.

Store Leadership

Founded by Sunil Kumar Maurya (Sonu)

E-Gadget World was established with a clear goal: to make premium, durable corporate laptops accessible to students, business professionals, educators, and households in Mehnajpur and surrounding areas in Azamgarh district.

Quality Protocol

Our Physical Laptop Testing Process

Every laptop in our commercial catalog undergoes thorough testing before handover to customers.

1. Display & Visual Check

Verification for dead pixels, backlight bleed, even color reproduction, and scratch-free screen panels.

2. CPU & RAM Stress Test

Multicore benchmark testing to ensure Intel Core i3 / i5 processors maintain turbo frequencies without thermal throttling.

3. SSD Read/Write Health

SMART storage health monitoring to confirm reliable read/write speeds on all solid-state drives.

4. Keyboard, Audio & Ports

Individual key actuation, trackpad responsiveness, speakers, webcam, Wi-Fi, and USB/HDMI ports are physically verified.
